# Re-processing pilot — 50 pages, 2026-11-08

Stratified sample, excluding the 16 eval pages and the 15 duplicates: 12
formerly rotated pages, 20 upright Feb–Apr, 12 upright Dec–Jan, 6 May.
Chain run exactly as the v0.4 routing does it: transcribe (Sonnet 5.5) →
metadata (Haiku 5.5) → first-pass + actions (Sonnet 5.5), each stage as
Claude Code subagents in batches of 10. Every replaced file is kept byte-exact
in `pages/<id>/revisions/<artifact>.2026-11-08.*`; snapshots also in `before/`.

Checked by eye on the worst disagreements: the new transcripts match the photos
word for word; the old ones were fluent invented text about the author's
projects that does not appear on the page. Many old Feb–Apr first-pass
summaries were a one-line placeholder. (Page-level detail stays in the
facility's copy of this report.)

## Downstream effect

- Project attribution changed on 43 of 50 pages, people on 25.
- Of the old first-pass entities, 27% survive on average.
- 10 pages carry a written date that differs from the page id (now logged in
  `meta.yaml` errors; `metadata.date` follows the page).
- 10 stale action files from the old chain were retired to revisions.
- Ledgers re-aggregated: 1,395 commitments (was 1,264), 64 people (was 44).
- The pilot pages feed 47 daily briefs, which are now out of date.

## Orientation, found on the way

The scanner trusted the old classifier's "upright" when a page had no red
series stamp. 27 pages were in that state; Sonnet 5.5 checked them all (27/27
where checked by eye; Haiku 5.5 got 23/27) and 11 were turned (7 upside down,
4 sideways), now fixed. The scanner now reports such pages as `unknown` until a
vision check confirms them, and classify moves to Sonnet.
